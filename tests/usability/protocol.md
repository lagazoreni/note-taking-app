# Usability protocol

## Scope

Evaluate SC-001, SC-002, and SC-010 for the local-first question workflow.

## Participants

Recruit at least 12 participants who take structured notes at least weekly. Do not
collect note content, account credentials, or identifying information. Participants
use a clean profile and the same seeded local dataset.

## Tasks

1. Create a workspace and a note with two inline questions.
2. Find both questions in Active Questions.
3. Link one existing question to a second note.
4. Answer and reopen a question.

Record first-attempt completion, elapsed time for tasks 1 and 3, assistance events,
and a five-point ease rating. The facilitator must not reveal control locations.

## Analysis

SC-001 passes when at least 90% complete task 1 without assistance. SC-002 passes
when median note capture is under 60 seconds and link insertion is under 30 seconds.
SC-010 passes when at least 85% rate the workflow 4 or 5. Report confidence limits
and exclude no participant after seeing results.
